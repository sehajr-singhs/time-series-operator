What the paper claims

  • 31/40 zero-shot series vs a per-series GRU token baseline (first positive median skill, +1.79 over persistence).
  • Geometry-first: Takens embedding → Koopman linearization → four temporal pretexts, no tokenizer anywhere.
  • Closed-form probe: rank-reduced least-squares Koopman fit in the frozen latent, zero gradient steps.
  • 128 months solar-cycle period recovered from a held-out channel (132 known, 3.0% error), stable at 128–130 across every checkpoint.
  • Label-free physics-vs-physiology test: top Koopman eigenvalue 0.999→0.937 (Lorenz) vs 0.692→0.207 (heartbeat).
  • Tipping detector fires at ρ≈21, before the homoclinic explosion at 24.74.

What it does not claim

  • Matched-compute negative: fine-tuning chronos-t5-small on the operator's own corpus at equal parameter-steps (52.0B) does not move it (21W/19L, p=0.98); 22× budget gains little (22W/18L, p=0.20).
  • Chronos-frozen still wins the head-to-head 24/40 (median +21.42 vs +1.79) — the gap is corpus breadth (80k vs 40 series).
  • So: at affordable compute, pretraining scale, not tokenization, decides. The operator must earn a comparable corpus before any paradigm claim.
